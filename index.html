What Is a Notary Public?

A notary public is a commissioned official authorized to perform specific notarial acts. One of the primary responsibilities of a notary is to verify the identity of the signer and complete the appropriate notarial certificate when the legal requirements are satisfied.

Depending on the document, the notarization may involve an acknowledgment, jurat, oath, affirmation, or another authorized notarial act.

It is important to understand that a notary public is not the same as an attorney. A notary can perform the authorized notarial act but does not provide legal advice or determine the legal effect of your document.
